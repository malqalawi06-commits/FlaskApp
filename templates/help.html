Instructions & Help

How to Use

  1. Enter a Research Topic (e.g., Agentic AI).
  2. Enter your Question for the AI Agent.
  3. Select how you want to sort the papers (Most Cited, Newest, etc.).
  4. Choose the number of papers to display.
  5. Click Search Papers to get results.

Example Questions for the AI Agent

  • Which paper is the most cited?
  • What is the newest paper?
  • Which paper has the least citations?
  • What is the oldest paper in these results?
