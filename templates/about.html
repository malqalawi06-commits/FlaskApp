About Smart Paper Explorer

This application is an intelligent academic research tool designed to help researchers explore and analyze scientific papers effectively.

What the Application Does

  • Academic Paper Search: Fetches research papers from Semantic Scholar based on your topic.
  • Citation Intelligence: Helps discover influential papers by analyzing citation counts and publication years.
  • AI Decision Making: Uses an AI Agent to interpret your questions and select the right tool to find answers.
  • Visitor Analytics: Displays connection and network details about the current session.
